# Submission Triage

This is a conservative contest-level ranking. A passing PoC proves that the behavior is reachable; it does not by itself prove that Sherlock will accept the behavior as an in-scope Medium. The probabilities below estimate acceptance as at least Medium, not whether the code behavior exists.

## Current Answer

The earlier shortlist contained four findings:

1. V-07: zero-share watermark reuse.
2. V-08: decay-rounding freeze.
3. V-09: decay-rate retroactivity.
4. V-01: confidence-unit compression.

That remains the correct original shortlist. Later work added four credible candidates: permissionless share-donation withdrawal censorship, stale updater authority after provider ownership transfer, the non-executable boundary quote sandwich, and the PriceVelocity same-block ratchet. The latest oracle-to-pool review added a strongly validated direct eight-decimal normalization loss and a contest-risky Chainlink liquidity-weighted bid/ask recentering issue. It also made V-07 more practical through a JIT/front-running sequence and materially strengthened V-06 with common provider parameters, although impact-attribution risk still keeps V-06 below the submission shortlist.

Realistically, the set is now:

- **Five clean Medium candidates:** direct oracle normalization loss, synthetic-ratio truncation, V-07, V-08, and V-09.
- **One solid but griefing-sensitive Medium candidate:** permissionless share-donation withdrawal censorship.
- **Five contest-risky Medium submissions:** Chainlink LWBA recentering, stale updater authority after ownership transfer, the boundary-quote sandwich, V-01, and the PriceVelocity ratchet.
- **No defensible Critical finding.**

## Submission Order

Submit the direct eight-decimal normalization loss first as Medium, followed by the synthetic-ratio precision loss, V-07, V-08, and V-09. Submit the share-donation withdrawal censorship next. Chainlink LWBA recentering now has a bounded asymmetric provider-path regression and is worth submitting as Medium, but describe the signed v3/HFS samples honestly and do not claim High without a signed triggering report. Follow with the stale-updater transition and boundary-quote sandwich. Submit V-01 narrowly around the inconsistent spread units. Submit PriceVelocity around the direct-versus-decomposed per-block cap bypass, not the weaker temporary reversal lock and not the known oracle-shock loss.

The realistic count is therefore **five strong Medium submissions, one solid but griefing-sensitive Medium submission, and five arguable Medium submissions**. Calling every candidate highly likely to pass would still not be supported by the contest assumptions or our own invalidation work.
